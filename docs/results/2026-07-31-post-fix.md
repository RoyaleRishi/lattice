# Post-Fix Regeneration — what the review fixes did to the published numbers

Task 12 deliverable of the `fix/review-findings` branch. Tasks 4, 5, 7, 8, 10,
10b and 10c changed measured quantities, which made every published benchmark
artifact stale. This document records, per track: **the before number, the
after number, the config that produced it, and a one-line reading.** No number
here was estimated, adjusted, or carried over from a brief — each was read out
of a file regenerated by the command in §9 against this repository's
checked-in code and data, on 2026-08-01, seed 0 throughout (GC3, GC8). The
"before" column is `git show` of the same file at commit `b6ada2a`.

Several results below are **worse for lattice** than what was published. They
are stated plainly, in the manner of commit `f34205d`.

## 1. Headline verdict

Four things changed, in descending order of how much they matter.

1. **The M5 redundancy headline does not survive a non-circular measurement.**
   The published "duplicate-rate 0.117 → 0.015 from `exact-label` to `nn@0.90`"
   was measured with the redundancy metric's threshold equal to the resolver's,
   which makes cosine-flagged duplicates impossible by construction. Measured
   with the thresholds decoupled, the same comparison is **0.7040 → 0.6769** on
   ConEL-2 — a 3.8 % relative reduction, not a 7.7× one. It is weaker still on
   MultiWOZ (0.9299 → 0.9102) (§5).
2. **The M3 identity claim reverses sign by corpus, and half of it does not
   survive a multiplicity correction.** A free core-dependency Snowball
   stemmer beats the shipped MiniLM embedding resolver on ConEL-2 and loses to
   it on ECB+, both significantly at the per-comparison level. Under Holm at
   α = 0.05 over the six-comparison family, both ConEL-2 comparisons involving
   `embedding-nn` fail (§3, §6).
3. **HCUKE was broken and is now merely mediocre.** f1@10 on Inspec goes
   0.1734 → 0.3029, clearing the frequency baseline at every k for the first
   time, and still finishing last of the three embedding-based scorers (§2).
4. **A large number of published "confidence intervals" were not confidence
   intervals.** M4 `edge-f1` and the ECB+ B³ cells now carry
   `brackets_estimate: false` and are labelled corpus-size sensitivity ranges.
   In the other direction, the ConEL-2 `ari` interval — which shipped sitting
   entirely *above* its own point estimate — now brackets correctly (§3.2, §4).

figure should be propagated.

## 3. M3 — identity resolution

### 3.1 The three-way identity ladder (T7) — new artifact

`PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m lattice.harness --sweep configs/m3-<corpus>-stemmed.toml reports/m3-<corpus>-stemmed`
(gold mentions, block units, `clustering` metric. ConEL-2 test: 58

manufactures fewer cross-document confusions), and the marginal B³-f1
size-dependence bias measures +0.042…+0.044 on ECB+ against +0.006…+0.011 on

manufactured by shrinkage; at `tau = 1` it is a property of the draws alone.

`brackets_estimate: true` is necessary, not sufficient — read the metric's own
docstring before quoting any of these as a CI.

### 3.3 Paired deltas across the ladder (T10b, T10c)

`PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python scripts/interval_analysis.py reports/intervals/analysis`
→ `reports/intervals/analysis/m3-paired-delta.json`. All three arms are
bootstrapped from the same seed over the same insertion-ordered document pool,
so iteration *i* draws identical document indices in every arm; the script
asserts the arms enumerate documents identically rather than assuming it.
`paired_delta` then rescales the size-*m* delta distribution by
`tau = sqrt(m/(n-m))`, and `prob_positive` is read off the *same* rescaled
distribution the band is built from.

The published version of this section reported **one** delta per corpus
(nn@0.90 − exact-label) from the n-out-of-n bootstrap. There are now three per
corpus, from the paired subsample path.

| corpus | comparison | before Δ / CI | after Δ | 95 % CI | P(Δ>0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| conel2 | nn@0.90 − exact-label | +0.0105 [0.0029, 0.0163] | **+0.0105** | **[+0.0023, +0.0160]** | 0.9864 |
| conel2 | stemmed − exact-label | *not published* | **+0.0251** | **[+0.0093, +0.0311]** | > 0.9999 |
| conel2 | nn@0.90 − stemmed | *not published* | **−0.0146** | **[−0.0220, −0.0004]** | 0.0184 |
| ecbplus | nn@0.90 − exact-label | +0.0171 [0.0096, 0.0216] | **+0.0171** | **[+0.0091, +0.0215]** | > 0.9999 |
| ecbplus | stemmed − exact-label | *not published* | **+0.0070** | **[+0.0026, +0.0102]** | 0.9997 |
| ecbplus | nn@0.90 − stemmed | *not published* | **+0.0101** | **[+0.0030, +0.0148]** | 0.9986 |

(m = 29, n = 58 on ConEL-2; m = 103, n = 206 on ECB+; B = 10000, seed 0.
`P(Δ>0)` values of `1.0000` in the JSON are written here as `> 0.9999`: with
B = 10000 draws that cell means zero exceedances, which bounds the tail at
1/(B+1) and no lower — see §6.)

**Reading: the `embedding-nn` vs `stemmed-label` comparison reverses sign by
corpus, significantly in both directions.** On ConEL-2 the stemmer wins by
0.0146 with P(Δ>0) = 0.0184; on ECB+ the embedding wins by 0.0101 with
P(Δ>0) = 0.9986. Both per-comparison CIs exclude zero, in opposite directions.
The embedding resolver's advantage over a free morphological baseline **does
not replicate across corpora**, and no honest statement of the identity result
can be made by picking one of the two.

It is worth noting which corpus is which. The architecture spec calls ConEL-2
"closest to the LLM-session framing" — the corpus most like lattice's stated
use case is the one where the core-dependency stemmer wins.

**Two margins are thin.** ConEL-2's nn − stemmed upper bound is **0.0004** from
zero, and ConEL-2's nn − exact P(Δ>0) is 0.9864, not the 1.000 the published
table carried. Both arise from the corrected rescale factor: at the harness's
`m = round(n/2)` the correct `tau = sqrt(m/(n-m))` is exactly 1.0 where the old
`sqrt(m/n)` was 0.707, so every subsample band is ~41 % wider than the same
draws produced before. No point estimate moved. §6 takes this further.

The two published deltas' *point estimates* are unchanged to four decimals;
only their intervals moved (ConEL-2 [0.0029, 0.0163] → [0.0023, 0.0160], ECB+
[0.0096, 0.0216] → [0.0091, 0.0215] — note this is two changes at once, the
scheme and the factor, since the published bands were n-out-of-n bootstrap).

The paired construction is well-posed even though the marginals are not
(§3.2): the size-dependence is shared by both arms and largely cancels in the
difference, and the delta draws straddle zero-difference in all six pairs.
(Task 10b measured the residual paired bias at −0.0008…−0.0052 on ECB+ against
a *marginal* bias of +0.042…+0.044; those two figures come from the execution
ledger, not from an artifact regenerated here, and are quoted as attribution
rather than as published numbers.)

## 5. M5 — intrinsic redundancy (T8)

`PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m lattice.harness --sweep configs/m5-<corpus>-sweep.toml reports/m5-<corpus>[-sweep]`

The published M5 redundancy numbers were measured with the metric's cosine
threshold **equal to** the resolver's (both 0.90). `embedding-nn` only admits a
new concept when its nearest neighbour is *below* its threshold, so at equal
thresholds no surviving pair can clear the metric's bar and
`cosine-duplicate-pairs` is **vacuously zero**. The sweep configs now set the
metric threshold to 0.60, below every point on the resolver axis
(0.65/0.75/0.90), so it measures something the resolver did not already
guarantee at every arm — the same threshold for all four arms, which is what
makes a before/after comparison legitimate.

**duplicate-rate, before → after, all three corpora:**

| resolver | ConEL-2 | MultiWOZ | ECB+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| embedding-nn@0.65 | 0.0000 → **0.2853** | 0.0000 → **0.4088** | 0.0000 → **0.3278** |
| embedding-nn@0.75 | 0.0000 → **0.5096** | 0.0000 → **0.7729** | 0.0000 → **0.5821** |
| embedding-nn@0.90 | 0.0153 → **0.6769** | 0.0193 → **0.9102** | 0.0116 → **0.7257** |
| exact-label | 0.1173 → **0.7040** | 0.3537 → **0.9299** | 0.1490 → **0.7556** |

**Reading:** the *direction* of the redundancy↔coherence tradeoff survives —
duplicate-rate still rises monotonically as the resolver merges less
aggressively, on all three corpora — but the *magnitude* of the improvement
lattice claims from its operating point does not. The `exact-label → nn@0.90`
reduction was 7.7× on ConEL-2, 18× on MultiWOZ and 12.8× on ECB+; measured
non-circularly it is 3.8 %, 2.1 % and 4.0 % respectively.

The absolute level is of course threshold-dependent: at 0.60 cosine MiniLM
flags a large fraction of all concept pairs, and MultiWOZ's task-dialogue
vocabulary saturates almost completely. That is an argument about how to read
the level, not about the comparison — both arms are measured at the same
threshold, which the shipped configuration was not.

At the *operating point* config (`configs/m5-conel2-nn090.toml`, metric
threshold 0.80 against resolver 0.90) the same run gives ConEL-2
`duplicate-rate` **0.2524**, `cosine-duplicate-pairs` **78**,
`label-duplicate-pairs` **4**, `near-duplicate-pairs` **79**, `concept-count`
**523** — against a published 0.0153 and 4 near-duplicate pairs.

`coherence`, `hierarchy-sanity` and `concept-count` are untouched by T8 and
reproduce exactly on all three corpora, including MultiWOZ's single 2-node
cycle and five transitive shortcuts. The Track 2 cross-corpus verdict — that
the tradeoff is a method property, not a ConEL-2 artifact — is unaffected; what
is affected is the size of the redundancy win being claimed.

## 6. Multiplicity — Holm at α = 0.05 over the six M3 comparisons

`reports/intervals/analysis/m3-multiplicity.json`, emitted by
`scripts/interval_analysis.py`. The six §3.3 deltas were published jointly with
no family-wise control, so each 95 % CI is a *per-comparison* statement only.
The published claim is about the shape of the identity ladder rather than about
any single pre-registered pair, which makes the family-wise error rate the
relevant quantity.

**Method, stated so it can be checked.** Holm-Bonferroni step-down over all six,
rather than plain Bonferroni: same FWER, uniformly more power, and no
independence assumption — which matters, because these six are heavily
dependent (three are differences among the same three arms on the same corpus,
and they satisfy an exact additive identity, (nn − exact) = (nn − stemmed) +
(stemmed − exact)).

**The two-sided p is not `2·min(P, 1−P)`.** `prob_positive` is the fraction of
B = 10000 rescaled paired draws above zero, so it has a resolution floor:
`P = 1.0000` means *no* draw out of 10000 fell the other way, not certainty.
The p-value uses the standard Monte Carlo `(r + 1) / (B + 1)` correction with
`r` the count on the near side, so a saturated cell floors at
p ≥ 2/10001 ≈ 2.0 × 10⁻⁴ and is reported as `< 2e-4`, never as zero.
Correspondingly, a reported `P(Δ>0) = 1.0000` should be read as
**> 1 − 1/(B+1) = 0.9999**, not as 1.

**Result.** Four of six survive; the two that fail are exactly the two ConEL-2
comparisons involving `embedding-nn`.

| rank | corpus | comparison | two-sided p | Holm threshold α/(k−i+1) | adjusted p | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | conel2 | stemmed − exact-label | < 2.0e-04 † | 0.008333 | 0.001200 | **survives** |
| 2 | ecbplus | nn@0.90 − exact-label | < 2.0e-04 † | 0.010000 | 0.001200 | **survives** |
| 3 | ecbplus | stemmed − exact-label | 0.000800 | 0.012500 | 0.003200 | **survives** |
| 4 | ecbplus | nn@0.90 − stemmed | 0.003000 | 0.016667 | 0.008999 | **survives** |
| 5 | conel2 | nn@0.90 − exact-label | 0.027397 | 0.025000 | 0.054795 | **fails** |
| 6 | conel2 | nn@0.90 − stemmed | 0.036996 | 0.050000 | 0.054795 | **fails** |

† at the Monte Carlo floor: zero exceedances in 10000 draws, so p is bounded at
2/10001 ≈ 1.9998e-04 and cannot be resolved further without a larger B.

**Reading.** Rank 5 fails on its own threshold (0.027397 > 0.025000). Rank 6 is
then killed by the step-down even though 0.036996 ≤ 0.050000 would pass it in
isolation — that is how Holm works, and it is the reason this table is
necessary rather than decorative. The consequence for the identity claim:

- **Survives family-wise control:** every ECB+ comparison, and `stemmed-label`
  beating `exact-label` on ConEL-2. So "morphological normalisation beats raw
  string matching" is solid on both corpora, and "the embedding beats the
  stemmer *on ECB+*" is solid.
- **Does not survive:** `embedding-nn@0.90` beating `exact-label` on ConEL-2,
  and the stemmer beating `embedding-nn@0.90` on ConEL-2. Neither ConEL-2
  claim about the embedding resolver may be stated without this qualification.

Note what this does and does not rescue. It does *not* restore the shipped
claim — the point estimate on ConEL-2 still favours the stemmer by 0.0146, and
the comparison that fails to reach family-wise significance is the *same
comparison* the README leans on in the other direction. The honest reading is
that ConEL-2 does not have the resolution to separate these three resolvers at
FWER 0.05 with 58 conversations, while ECB+, with 206 documents, does.

The published documents apply no multiplicity correction at all; this is new
analysis, not a re-derivation.
